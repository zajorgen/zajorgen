# Hey I'm Zach Jorgensen

### My Story
I'm a student at Brigham Young University studying Statistical Science. I love learning and discovering new thing through data! I'm using this profile to showcase some of my personal and class projects that I think were pretty interesting. 

### My Projects

##### 1. Rats in NYC
I worked on a case competition here at my school. We had to help the city of New York find out what factors of a City Council district were associated with higher rodent issues. We ended up winning 2nd place! Some of the code I personally worked on (Rodents_EDA_zach's_stuff.R) and the final presentation (Ratatouille.pptx) are here in the repository. 

##### 2. Top Golf
I love going to Top Golf with my friends but I'm not much of a golfer. I designed an experiment to see which clubs would help me perform better. The data I collected (TopGolf_Data.csv) and my final report (TopGolf Project Report.qmd) can be found in the Sports_Analytics section of the repository. 

##### 3. College Basketball
In preparation for March Madness in 2025, I did some exploratory analysis on several teams in who would be competing. Check out my findings (College Bball.R) in the Sports_Analytics section of the repository.

##### 4. MLB Hitting
While watching the Yankees in the World Series a few years ago, I began to wonder how Aaron Judge hit so many homeruns despite being super tall and having a larger strike zone. About a year later, I remembered this question and decided to investigate what effect height and weight had on MLB performance. You can see the analysis (MLB_Player_Analysis.R) in the Sports_Analytics section of this repository.
